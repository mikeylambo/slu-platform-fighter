The SLU Platform Fighter content-pack tooling, validation workflow, provenance discipline, and animation-pipeline structure were implemented after review of Super Bash Folds' MIT-licensed fighter/stage pack system and open fighter pipeline. The SLU implementation substantially extends and changes that model for deterministic cross-engine gameplay data, 3D-at-runtime fighters, a broader animation contract, lifecycle certification, semantic materials/sockets, and Blender-based GLB asset auditing.

Super Bash Folds' combat engine, float-based simulation, sprite-atlas runtime, roster content, and third-party assets are not incorporated into the SLU kernel.
